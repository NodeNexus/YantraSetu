2. Support We Are Seeking from the Seva Innovation Challenge

  • Pilot Deployment Site: Opportunity to mount initial 2–3 node trial kits in a municipal water pumping station (e.g. Nagpur Municipal Corporation), an MSRTC bus maintenance workshop, or a local MIDC manufacturing cluster.
  • Grant Funding for Industrial Sensor Hardware: ₹65,000 to ₹1,20,000 for acquiring high-temperature industrial accelerometers, IP65 enclosures, and calibration equipment.
  • Domain Mentorship: Guidance from senior mechanical reliability engineers and vibration analysts on field-tuning ISO 10816 vibration severity standards.
