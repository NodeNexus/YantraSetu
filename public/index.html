Engineering Depth

Why Single-Sensor Monitoring Fails MSMEs

Rotating machines undergo distinct physical degradation phases. Relying solely on vibration or temperature causes late detection or false alarms.

🔥

Thermal Infrared (MLX90640)

32×24 Far-Infrared thermopile array captures non-contact surface heat distribution. Identifies electrical winding hotspot degradation, poor motor ventilation, and severe friction before bearing seizure occurs.

• Model: MobileNetV3-Small (96.1% top-3)
🔊

Vibration Dynamics (20 kHz)

High-kHz contact accelerometer mounted on the Drive-End bearing housing. Computes RMS velocity and Kurtosis to isolate microscopic impact blows caused by bearing race spalls and shaft unbalance.

• Model: NASA IMS Bearing Random Forest
🎧

Acoustic Sound (16 kHz MEMS)

Directional acoustic microphone captures airborne chattering, valve blow-by, and pump cavitation. Extracts 61 spectral and psychoacoustic MFCC features to catch micro-cracks before thermal rise starts.

• Model: L2 Regularized Classifier (97.6% recall)
🛡

The Core Differentiator: Layered False-Alarm Confidence Gate

In industrial condition monitoring, a false alarm that halts a production line is nearly as costly as a real defect. YantraSetu solves this with a three-tier gating protocol:

1. Ambient Normalization

Dynamic baseline floating that accounts for 44°C Vidarbha summer heatwaves without tripping alarms.

2. Temporal Persistence

Requires an anomaly to persist across consecutive rolling sampling frames before triggering warnings.

3. Transparent "Learning" State

Displays "Unconfirmed / Learning" rather than a false trip when a model is statistically uncertain.

Implementation Trajectory

12-Month Field Deployment Roadmap

A clear, phased execution plan taking YantraSetu from university benchtop validation to active shop-floor deployments in Nagpur’s industrial clusters.

PHASE 1: MONTHS 0 – 3

Benchtop Motor Instrumentation

Mount physical MLX90640, accelerometer, and microphone onto a 5 HP test induction motor in the RBU Mechanical Engineering Lab. Calibrate ambient floating baseline across morning and afternoon heat cycles.

PHASE 2: MONTHS 3 – 6

Controlled Fault Seeding

Artificially introduce calibrated mechanical defects: 0.5 mm angular shaft misalignment, dynamic rotor unbalance masses, and outer-race EDM notches. Fine-tune the Confidence Gate under background shop noise.

PHASE 3: MONTHS 6 – 12

Industrial Shop-Floor Pilot

Deploy 3 pilot monitoring kits on active rotating machinery in partner facilities: targeting Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC) water pump stations, an MSRTC bus maintenance depot, or MIDC Hingna pump units.
