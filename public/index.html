1. Executive Summary

YantraSetu is an affordable, edge-computing condition monitoring system designed specifically for micro, small, and medium manufacturing enterprises (MSMEs). By continuously observing a rotating machine’s thermal emission (infrared), vibration dynamics, and acoustic noise (sound), YantraSetu identifies early warning signs of component degradation (bearing flaking, shaft misalignment, unbalance, stator overheating) long before catastrophic mechanical failure occurs.

"Transitioning industrial maintenance from reactive emergency fire-fighting to proactive planned repair—without requiring lakhs in proprietary SCADA investments."

2. The Problem in Indian Manufacturing

Rotating machinery—including electric motors, centrifugal pumps, conveyor gearboxes, and industrial blowers—forms the core of manufacturing operations across India's industrial belts (such as MIDC Hingna and Butibori in Vidarbha, Maharashtra).

  • Sudden Unplanned Breakdowns: Critical machines fail without warning, halting production lines, causing worker safety risks, and costing small units tens of thousands of rupees per downtime incident.
  • Inadequate Manual Audits: Today, small plants rely on periodic manual rounds where a technician feels a motor with a palm or uses an isolated vibration pen once a month. Nascent defects are routinely missed.
  • Prohibitive Commercial Costs: Imported commercial condition monitoring systems cost between ₹3,00,000 and ₹15,00,000 per installation and demand dedicated vibration vibration analysts to interpret complex FFT spectra.

3. The YantraSetu Solution

YantraSetu bridges the gap between manual guesswork and high-cost industrial IoT. It packages tri-modal sensing onto an inexpensive edge computer (Raspberry Pi 4) that operates directly on the factory floor without requiring expensive recurring cloud subscriptions.

Parameter Traditional Approach YantraSetu Edge System
Sensing Modalities Single signal (often spot vibration or periodic IR gun) Tri-Modal: Thermal Video + High-kHz Vibration + Acoustic Sound
