2. Economic Return on Investment (ROI) for Indian MSMEs

Consider a typical small auto-component or plastic moulding unit in MIDC Hingna running 10 machine lines:

  • Average Unplanned Downtime Cost: ₹15,000 to ₹35,000 per hour (idle labor, missed dispatch penalties, ruined raw material).
  • Cost of Single Catastrophic Motor Burnout: ₹45,000 (replacement + rewind + 8 hours downtime).
  • YantraSetu Initial Investment: Approx. ₹21,000 one-time hardware per critical machine.
  • Payback Period: Less than 2 months if just one catastrophic line failure is avoided.
