The 12-Month Field Implementation Plan

MONTHS 0 – 3

Phase 1: Lab Test Motor Setup

Mount physical MLX90640, accelerometer, and microphone onto a 5 HP test induction motor in the university mechanical lab. Verify continuous 72-hour zero-leak telemetry streaming over I2C and SPI buses.

MONTHS 3 – 6

Phase 2: Controlled Fault Seeding

Artificially induce calibrated physical defects: 0.5 mm angular shaft misalignment, dynamic rotor unbalance masses, and outer-race bearing EDM micro-notches. Validate false-alarm immunity under shop noise.

MONTHS 6 – 12

Phase 3: MSME Shop-Floor Trials

Deploy 3 ruggedized pilot nodes in partner manufacturing workshops in MIDC Hingna/Butibori and public utilities (NMC municipal water pumping station), monitoring real operating machines for 6 continuous months.
