2. The Three Modalities Explained

Modality A: Infrared Thermal Imaging

Role: Detects friction-induced heat buildup, inadequate bearing lubrication, electrical stator insulation degradation, and cooling fin clogging.

  • Sensor: MLX90640 Far-Infrared sensor (32 × 24 thermopile array, 55° or 110° Field of View).
  • Processing: Spatial temperature map analysis extracting maximum surface hotspot, mean casing temperature, and thermal gradient relative to ambient.
  • Model: MobileNetV3-Small deep learning model trained on thermal motor images, achieving 96.1% top-3 accuracy across distinct operating conditions.

Modality B: High-Frequency Vibration Analysis

Role: Serves as the primary indicator for mechanical rotating defects—identifying bearing outer-race cracks, inner-race flaking, ball defect passing frequencies, and rotor unbalance.

  • Sensor: Industrial contact accelerometer mounted via magnetic stud to the motor drive-end (DE) bearing housing.
  • Processing: High-speed 20 kHz sampling capturing acceleration waveforms. Features include Root Mean Square (RMS) acceleration, Kurtosis (impulsiveness of impact blows), and Crest Factor.
  • Model: Baseline statistical calibration combined with Random Forest classification calibrated on NASA IMS run-to-failure benchmarks.

Modality C: Acoustic Noise Emission

Role: Captures airborne mechanical chattering, high-frequency cavitation in pumps, valve leakage, and acoustic friction preceding measurable thermal rise.

  • Sensor: Directional MEMS microphone enclosed in a directional acoustic baffle.
  • Processing: 16 kHz audio streaming decomposed into 61 frequency and psychoacoustic features (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients, spectral centroid, spectral rolloff, and octave band energies).
  • Model: L2-regularized acoustic anomaly detector achieving 97.6% recall on industrial sound benchmarks.

3. The Innovation: The Layered Confidence Gate

The primary operational flaw of early-generation machine health monitors on Indian shop floors is false alarm fatigue. When a tool drops near a motor or ambient factory temperatures spike at midday in Nagpur (exceeding 44°C), conventional threshold alarms trigger false plant shutdowns. Operators quickly disconnect the sensors in frustration.

YantraSetu resolves this through a three-stage gating mechanism:

  1. Ambient Normalization: Thermal thresholds dynamically adapt to ambient workshop temperatures rather than applying rigid static cutoffs.
  2. Temporal Confirmation: An anomaly must persist across multiple consecutive measurement windows before escalating from Investigating to Warning.
  3. Transparent "Not Sure" State: When sensor confidence drops below mathematical reliability thresholds, the system flags "Learning / Evaluating" instead of crying wolf.
